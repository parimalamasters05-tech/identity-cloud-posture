How findings are scored

Severity comes from an explicit impact × exposure matrix, then three modifiers. Nothing here is a proprietary score — the arithmetic for every finding is in the evidence appendix, and we expect you to check it.

risk score = base(severity) × privilege × blast radius × confidence

TermMeaning
Impact (1–5)How much damage this enables if exploited, from negligible to organization-wide compromise.
Exposure (1–5)How reachable the weakness is, from requiring local access to reachable from the internet with no sign-in.
Privilege×{{ matrix.modifier('privileged_entity') }} when any affected account holds administrative rights. The same gap on a super-administrator outranks it on a contractor.
Blast radiusGrows with the number of affected accounts, but sub-linearly and capped at ×{{ matrix.modifier('blast_radius_cap') }}. A gap affecting forty people is worse than one affecting four, not ten times worse.
ConfidenceReduces the score where a finding is inferred rather than read directly from an authoritative field.

Ordering of the action plan

The priority action plan lists critical items first, then high, then medium and low. Within each severity, items are ordered by risk removed per hour of effort, so the cheap, high-value fixes come first — in a small team the work that actually gets done is the work that fits in an afternoon. A quick fix never jumps ahead of a more severe problem. Effort estimates scale with the number of accounts or files involved where the fix is done one at a time.
